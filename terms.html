What is free

These parts of Dozy are free forever: one alarm, one mission per alarm, the three free missions (Smile at Dozy, Shake Dozy awake, Count the acorns), three alarm sounds, streaks, the progress calendar, the share card, the Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, the starter decor and most of the burrow shop.

Acorns are earned by getting up. They cannot be bought, sold or exchanged, and they have no cash value.

Dozy Plus

Dozy Plus adds unlimited alarms, all nine missions, up to three missions on one alarm, the wake-up check, five more alarm sounds and a few Plus decor items. There are three ways to get it:

These are US prices. The price in your country is shown in the app before you buy.

The free trial

The yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial. You are not charged during the trial. When the trial ends, the yearly subscription starts and you are charged the yearly price, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Apple offers the trial once, to new subscribers.

Renewal and cancelling

  • Yearly and monthly subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account.
  • To stop a renewal, cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • To manage or cancel: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • If you cancel, Dozy Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid for. After that, the free parts of Dozy keep working.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

"Restore purchases" is in the paywall and in Settings. Use it after you reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone.

Please read this before you rely on an alarm

Dozy is an aid to getting up. It is not a guarantee that you will wake up. It uses Apple's AlarmKit and needs iOS 26 or later, and there are things neither Dozy nor we control. An alarm may not ring, or may be too quiet to wake you, if:

  • Alarms are not allowed for Dozy in the Settings app
  • Your volume is low
  • Your iPhone is switched off or out of battery

Before your first real morning, use "Test my alarm". It rings your alarm in 10 seconds, through the same path as a real alarm, so you can check the permission and the volume.

Do not rely on Dozy as your only safeguard for something critical, such as a flight, an exam or a shift you cannot miss. Set a second alarm on something else.

An alarm in Dozy can always be ended. Holding "Hold to give up" for 3 seconds ends it, and it gives up by itself after 10 returns or 45 minutes. An alarm that has ended this way does not come back, so do not count on it after that.

Dozy is an alarm clock. It is not a medical device, and it makes no claims about your health or your sleep. Some missions ask you to move (walking, squats). Do them only if they are safe for you. You can always pick a different mission.
